About — one maker, one bench
Jakarta, small bench

One maker, one bench.

Saltframe is one person, one bench, one pair at a time. Frames are cut from cellulose acetate sheets, tumbled overnight and polished by hand — the slow part is the point.

The brand name is temporary and this site is a demo: the shapes, prices and archive here are sample data. What is real is the approach — a conversation before a checkout, a confirmed request before a single cut.

No stock, no batches, no shelf of yesterday's designs waiting for a buyer. You describe the face and the use; the bench answers with one pair, engraved SALTFRAME on the inside of the temple.
