Process — request to doorstep
~10–14 working days (demo)

How it works.

A conversation, not a checkout. You send one request; a person reads it, replies, and only then does the cutting start. One pair at a time.

01

Request

Pick a frame, a material and a lens. Leave your name, email and a short note — face width, prescription, anything that matters.

Day 0 — send
02

Confirm

We reply by person: shape check, final material, lens choice, demo quote. Nothing is cut before you say yes.

Day 1–2 — reply
03

Cut & polish

Front and temples cut from the sheet, tumbled overnight, hand-polished on the wheel. Lens cut and mounted, temples bent to your head.

Day 3–10 — bench
04

QC & ship

Alignment check, hinge tension, engraving inside the temple, then packed and sent with a tracking number.

Day 10–14 — send

What we need from you

FrameTide, Dune, or Moss — or a note describing your own
MaterialBlack / Tortoise / Olive
LensClear / Sun / Blue light (+ prescription numbers, if any)
ContactName + email — we reply to that address
OptionalFace width, current frame measurements, deadline
