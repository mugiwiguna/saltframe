Care & guarantee.

Acetate ages well when it is treated like a tool, not a toy. A minute a day keeps the polish and the hinge honest.

Daily cleaning

Lukewarm water and one drop of mild soap. Rinse, shake off, dry with a lint-free cloth — never wipe dry grit across the lens.

Storage

Case when it is not on your face. Never on the car dashboard, never lens-down on a table.

Adjustments

Hinges loosened? Use both hands, grip at the hinge, nudge gently. Or send it back — realigning is part of the ten years.

Avoid

Solvents, alcohol wipes, hot water, hairspray directly on the frame. The polish forgives none of them.
